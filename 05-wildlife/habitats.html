01 / THE WORLD’S ECOSYSTEMS

Where life finds a way

HABITAT / 01

Tropical Rainforests

Warm, humid forests with high rainfall and multiple canopy layers.

Climate
Warm temperatures; frequent heavy rain.
Wildlife
Orangutans, hornbills, frogs and insects.
Why it matters
Store carbon, regulate rainfall and support immense biodiversity.
HABITAT / 02

Grasslands and Savannas

Expansive open landscapes dominated by grasses and scattered trees.

Climate
Seasonal rainfall; dry periods and occasional fires.
Wildlife
Lions, zebras, giraffes and antelope.
Why it matters
Support migration, grazing cycles and nutrient-rich soils.
HABITAT / 03

Deserts

Dry ecosystems with plants and animals adapted to scarce water.

Climate
Low precipitation; often large daily temperature changes.
Wildlife
Foxes, reptiles, rodents and camels.
Why it matters
Support specialized species and unique food webs.
HABITAT / 04

Mountains

High-elevation landscapes with steep slopes and distinct vegetation zones.

Climate
Cooler at altitude; snow in many high ranges.
Wildlife
Snow leopards, mountain goats and eagles.
Why it matters
Supply freshwater and provide climate refuges.
HABITAT / 05

Wetlands

Marshes, swamps and floodplains where water shapes life.

Climate
Seasonal or permanent waterlogging.
Wildlife
Herons, frogs, fish and otters.
Why it matters
Filter water, reduce flooding and shelter young wildlife.
HABITAT / 06

Polar Regions

Ice-covered and tundra landscapes at Earth’s northern and southern extremes.

Climate
Very cold, with long winter darkness.
Wildlife
Polar bears in the Arctic; penguins in Antarctica.
Why it matters
Influence global climate and sustain polar food webs.
02 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Why protect natural habitats?

01

Prevent habitat loss

Protected land and sustainable planning reduce fragmentation.

02

Protect biodiversity

Connected habitats allow species to feed, breed and migrate.

03

Maintain balance

Healthy ecosystems sustain soil, water and food chains.

04

Help endangered species

Safeguarding critical habitat gives threatened populations room to recover.
