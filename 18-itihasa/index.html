THE SPIRIT OF THE EPICS

Eight foundational pillars of Indian consciousness that guide human destiny through the trials of time.

धर्मः

Dharma

Righteousness, cosmic order, and sacred duty that sustains society and human conscience.

भक्तिः

Devotion

Unconditional love, surrender, and pure reverence exemplified by Sita, Hanuman, and Vidura.

शौर्यम्

Courage

Fearless fortitude to stand for justice against insurmountable odds, as seen in Arjuna and Abhimanyu.

त्यागः

Sacrifice

Renunciation of self-interest for the greater welfare, embodied in Rama’s exile and Bhishma’s vow.

निष्ठा

Loyalty

Unshakable fidelity to truth and kinship, shown in Lakshmana’s vigil and Karna’s friendship.

ज्ञानम्

Wisdom

Transcendental insight into the nature of the self, revealed in the Bhagavad Gita and Valmiki’s verses.

न्यायः

Justice

The inexorable karmic balance where Adharma falls and righteous order is restored to the earth.

करुणा

Compassion

Universal mercy toward all living beings, granting shelter even to repentant foes.

ICONIC MOMENTS OF THE EPICS

Witness the pivotal turning points where destinies were shaped and immortal lessons etched into human memory.

Ramayana

Rama's Exile to the Forest

Upholding his father’s sacred promise, Lord Rama renounces the imperial throne of Ayodhya to live in the Dandakaranya forest for fourteen years.

Ramayana

The Building of Rama Setu

Millions of Vanaras write the sacred name of 'RAMA' on stones, constructing a miraculous floating bridge across the roaring ocean to Lanka.

Mahabharata

Draupadi's Swayamvara

Disguised as a young Brahmana, Arjuna pierces the eye of the revolving fish target through its reflection in water, winning Princess Draupadi.

Mahabharata

The Bhagavad Gita on Kurukshetra

Between the two poised armies, Lord Krishna imparts the 700 verses of transcendental wisdom to dispirit Arjuna, illuminating the paths of Yoga.

Ramayana

The Coronation & Deepotsava

Returning victorious after fourteen years, Lord Rama and Sita are crowned in Ayodhya, inaugurating the golden age of Ramrajya amidst millions of lamps.

Mahabharata

The Duel of Karna and Arjuna

On Day 17 of Kurukshetra, the long-destined confrontation between the two peerless archers of ancient Bharat reaches its fateful, tragic climax.
