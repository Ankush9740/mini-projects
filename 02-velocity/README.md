# VELOCITY — The Art of Speed

Five-page static automotive showcase built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP and ScrollTrigger.

## Open
Open `index.html` directly in a browser. An internet connection is required for remote fonts, GSAP CDN and Unsplash photographs.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `collection.html` — Collection
- `details.html` — Porsche 911 GT3 RS editorial detail
- `innovation.html` — Engineering and technology
- `contact.html` — About and frontend-only contact form

## Important notes
Images are remote editorial Unsplash photos. Their subjects should be visually checked before public use; a photo is not proof of a precise model/trim. Some images are reused, and the detail gallery labels alternate editorial imagery. Manufacturer specs are representative and should be verified against the relevant model-year brochure before publication. Contact form only validates in the browser; it does not send data. Page animations require the GSAP CDN to load. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
