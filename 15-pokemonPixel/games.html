POKÉMON FIRERED VERSION

REGION: KANTO

Step back into the shoes of the iconic trainer from Pallet Town. Pokémon FireRed modernized the revolutionary 1996 adventure with 32-bit pixel artwork, wireless connectivity, and an expanded Sevii Islands storyline. Foil Giovanni’s Team Rocket syndicate across mainland Kanto and battle your fierce rival for the championship title.

STARTER POKÉMON
LEGENDARY MASCOT
Charizard
KEY INNOVATIONS & FEATURES
  • Bundled with the revolutionary Game Boy Advance Wireless Adapter.
  • The Sevii Islands post-game expansion featuring Johto wild Pokémon.
  • Fame Checker key item for logging lore about Gym Leaders and the Elite Four.
  • Teachy TV visual tutorial system for novice trainers.
RELEASED: SEPTEMBER 2004
GAME FREAK • NINTENDO

POKÉMON LEAFGREEN VERSION

REGION: KANTO

Released side-by-side with FireRed, LeafGreen celebrates the timeless heritage of Pokémon Green. Trainers navigate lush trails, battle through the legendary Rock Tunnel and Pokémon Tower, and confront the Elite Four atop Indigo Plateau.

STARTER POKÉMON
LEGENDARY MASCOT
Venusaur
KEY INNOVATIONS & FEATURES
  • Exclusive wild encounters: Sandshrew, Vulpix, Bellsprout, Slowpoke, Staryu, and Magmar.
  • Inter-game trading with Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, and Colosseum.
  • Enhanced Gen 3 battle system, Abilities, and EV/IV mechanics.
  • Charming Union Room mini-games and wireless chat system.
RELEASED: MAY 2005
GAME FREAK • NINTENDO

POKÉMON EMERALD VERSION

REGION: HOENN

Widely heralded as the magnum opus of the GBA era. Emerald merges the dual storylines of Ruby and Sapphire into one grand cataclysm. As the ancient weather leviathans tear apart Sootopolis City, trainers climb the treacherous Sky Pillar to summon Rayquaza and end the storm.

STARTER POKÉMON
LEGENDARY MASCOT
Rayquaza
KEY INNOVATIONS & FEATURES
  • The legendary Battle Frontier: 7 battle facilities overseen by Frontier Brains (Battle Dome, Battle Pike, Battle Pyramid, Battle Factory...).
  • Fully animated front battle sprites for all Pokémon upon entering battle.
  • Concurrent battles with both Team Magma and Team Aqua.
  • Gym Leader rematches via the PokéNav Match Call feature.
RELEASED: MARCH 2003
GAME FREAK • NINTENDO

POKÉMON RUBY VERSION

REGION: HOENN

Pokémon Ruby initiated the third generation with unprecedented ambition. Introducing vibrant weather effects, 135 completely new Pokémon, Secret Bases, and Double Battles, it redefined what a handheld RPG could deliver. Confront Maxie and Team Magma as they attempt to expand the earth's continents.

STARTER POKÉMON
LEGENDARY MASCOT
Groudon
KEY INNOVATIONS & FEATURES
  • Introduction of Pokémon Abilities and Natures.
  • Double Battles: commanding two Pokémon side-by-side simultaneously.
  • Pokémon Contests across Cool, Beauty, Cute, Smart, and Tough categories.
  • Secret Bases customizable with furniture, dolls, and mats via Secret Power.
RELEASED: MARCH 2003
GAME FREAK • NINTENDO

POKÉMON SAPPHIRE VERSION

REGION: HOENN

Pokémon Sapphire pairs with Ruby to offer a maritime odyssey. Journey through underwater oceanic trenches with HM Dive, discover submerged ancient shipwrecks, and halt Archie and Team Aqua from drowning Hoenn in a perpetual deluge.

STARTER POKÉMON
LEGENDARY MASCOT
Kyogre
KEY INNOVATIONS & FEATURES
  • Underwater diving mechanics with deep sea currents and submerged caverns.
  • Exclusive wild Pokémon: Lotad, Lombre, Ludicolo, Sableye, Seviper, and Lunatone.
  • PokéNav navigator with Hoenn Town Map, Condition viewer, and Trainer's Eyes.
  • Acro Bike and Mach Bike cycling dynamics.
