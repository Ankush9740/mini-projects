SHINOBI Chronicles is a non-commercial, unofficial fan-made website celebrating the characters, themes and world-building of Naruto.

It is a frontend showcase built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP and ScrollTrigger. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the franchise’s owners.
