EMERGING DIRECTIONS

Signals to watch.

Progress is uneven. Each direction brings opportunities alongside reliability, safety, and governance questions.

Agentic workflows

Systems that break goals into steps, use software tools, and coordinate tasks with checkpoints and human approval.

Physical & embodied intelligence

Robots operating in dynamic environments by combining perception, planning, and control.

Efficient on-device models

AI running closer to users, potentially improving latency, cost, and privacy when designed well.

AI for science

Models assisting with materials discovery, simulation, and complex scientific datasets.

Evaluation & trustworthy AI

Methods for measuring reliability, robustness, interpretability, security, and real-world performance.
