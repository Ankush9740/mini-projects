# NEURAL/ATLAS — A Field Guide to Artificial Intelligence

A five-page responsive static website covering major AI families, real-world applications, and emerging directions.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Overview
- `types.html` — AI taxonomy with interactive category filters
- `applications.html` — Applications across industries
- `frontier.html` — Emerging research and technology directions
- `contact.html` — About and frontend-validated contact form

## Technology
HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, GSAP 3 and ScrollTrigger via CDN. No frameworks, build step, backend, or database.

## Run
Extract the ZIP and open `index.html` in a modern browser. Internet access is required for Google Fonts, GSAP CDN, and remote Unsplash photographs.

## Notes
The contact form validates fields in the browser only; it does not transmit or store messages. AI capabilities evolve quickly; this is introductory educational content. External image availability may change.
