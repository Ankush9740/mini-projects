COMBAT TIPS

  • Chain Punch → Punch → Kick to build huge combos and launch foes.
  • Collect blue Ki Orbs and green Senzu Orbs dropped by defeated enemies.
  • Fill Ki to 100% and press T (or hold Shift) to unleash Super Saiyan: +45% power, faster movement, and Golden Kamehameha!
  • Defeat each stage's Boss to advance through all 4 battlefields.
