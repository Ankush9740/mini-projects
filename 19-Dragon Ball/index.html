COMBAT TIPS

  • Chain Punch → Punch → Kick to build combos and fill your SSJ Transformation Meter.
  • Collect blue Ki Orbs and green Senzu Orbs dropped by defeated enemies and cracked boulders.
  • When the SSJ bar reaches 100%, press T to transform into Super Saiyan for 18 seconds: +45% power, faster movement & combos, and Enhanced Golden Kamehameha!
  • Defeat each stage's Boss to advance through all 4 battlefields.
